# Goodbye Goth Project State

Current version: v0.4

## User-approved structure
- 12 levels.
- Every level: exploration, Zombie Goth encounters, one Relationship Challenge, one Puzzle/Question, one Queen boss.
- Boss gate unlocks only after required objectives are complete.
- Difficulty grows with more enemies, more puzzles, harder enemy behaviours, more complex maps and stronger boss mechanics.

## Intro
1. Goodnight: Olly and Scarlett together, goodnight kiss.
2. Huh?: black screen, blink / blink blink.
3. Where's Scarlett?: first-person wording: "You wake up..."
4. The Note: Goths took the princess; Begin Quest enters Level 1.

## Cast
Olly: curly dark hair, The Weeknd shirt, olive-green cargos, bow and arrows. Exploration view can primarily show his curly-haired back; combat shows his face.
Princess Scarlett: based on provided photos, brown hair, glasses, pink princess styling, long-dress influence, tiara. The tiara remains with her when kidnapped and may be slightly lopsided.

## Zombie Goth rule
Zombie Goths are distinct from all Queen bosses. They are simplified undead creatures with their own silhouettes, dialogue and attack mechanics. Do not reuse Queen art as ordinary enemies.

## Relationship events
One bespoke challenge per level. Designed progressively, not continuous. Level 8 must have both a real Relationship Challenge and a separate Weeknd/Lorde question.

## Ending
After Luna: rescue Scarlett, cute wedding, congratulations card, then a tiny goth appears with the final warning that a goth is always lurking. END.
