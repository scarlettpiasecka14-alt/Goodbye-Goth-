
# Goodbye Goth Project State

Current version: v0.2

## Locked story structure
Four-click opening:
1. GOODNIGHT: Olly and Scarlett share a goodnight and a kiss.
2. HUH?: black screen, blink / blink blink.
3. WHERE'S SCARLETT?: first-person text, "You wake up..."
4. THE NOTE: the Goths have taken Princess Scarlett; BEGIN QUEST starts Level 1.

## Level structure
Every level contains:
- exploration with a map that reveals as Olly travels
- required Zombie Goth fights
- one unique Relationship Challenge
- one separate Puzzle / Question
- a boss gate that only opens when required objectives are complete
- one unique Goth Queen boss

## Zombie Goth rule
Zombie Goths are a separate enemy family. They must NOT visually or mechanically duplicate the Queens.
Zombie Goths are creature-like, more simplified and undead, with distinct attacks and difficulty.
Queens are the named, unique, fully designed characters.

## Main characters
Olly:
- likeness based on provided reference photos and approved storyboard
- The Weeknd shirt
- olive green cargos

- gameplay can use rear-facing curly-head view for exploration

Princess Scarlett:
- likeness based on provided photos
- brown hair
- glasses

Luna:
- separate fictional final boss
- icy blonde / blue / cold gothic aesthetic
- surprise reveal in Level 12

## Relationship events
There are 12 total, one per level, developed as each level is built.

The exact event design is allowed to change as the level gets polished.
Level 8 must include both a relationship challenge AND a separate Weeknd/Lorde question.
