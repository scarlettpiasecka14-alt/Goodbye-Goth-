# Goodbye Goth Project State

## Core premise
Olly must rescue Princess Scarlett after the Goths kidnap her. There are 12 levels. Every level contains exploration, multiple fights, puzzles/challenges, and a boss fight. Defeating the boss unlocks the next level.

## Final ending
Level 12 is followed by the reveal of the final goth: Luna. Luna is an inside-joke fictional boss reveal, not a real-life attack. After defeating Luna:
1. Olly rescues Scarlett.
2. Cute wedding sequence.
3. End card: "Congratulations, you rescued your princess... but don't get too comfortable. A goth is always lurking."
4. A tiny goth pops up.
5. END.

## Visual direction
Current storyboard is the master visual reference. It is a cute, dark, hand-drawn / 2D indie-game aesthetic. Olly and Scarlett should remain recognisable and consistent across story panels, gameplay, encounters and the wedding scene.

## Goth guide source
Five core goth types:
1. Classic Goth
2. Chaos Fairy Goth
3. Satanist Goth
4. Pink Princess Goth
5. Poser / E-Girl Goth

The uploaded "Goth Girl Guide" is the canonical source for their traits, habitats, warning signs and jokes.
