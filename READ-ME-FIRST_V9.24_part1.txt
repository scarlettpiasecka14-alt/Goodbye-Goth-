WHAT CHANGED
 - Zombie Goth: new creepier walk, new more detailed death, new 3-2-1 fight-stance animation (she now stays in
   the last pose of it as her "ready" stance).
 - Chain Goth: new walk and death. The old yellow SPIN is replaced by the SLAM:
     * she steps in until Olly is about a jump away, always facing him when she starts;
     * 1st slam = warning only (no damage), the yellow floor strip lights up;
     * 2nd slam = a wave of sparks sweeps across the floor in the direction she is facing (15 damage);
     * JUMP OVER HER (land behind her) or be in the air when it goes off = safe.
       Standing in front of her on the ground = hit. Dodging does NOT avoid it.
 - Olly's knockout: the "ugh" plays as he drops, then a thud as he hits the floor.
 - Map zoom 1.7 -> 1.5.

TESTING CHECKLIST (Safari, Mac)
 1. Hard-refresh first (Cmd+Shift+R). Fight the Zombie Goth: 3-2-1 animation plays, she shambles toward you,
    feet sit on the floor, she faces you; claw/bite attacks still work; she collapses when beaten and stays down.
 2. Chain Goth: new walk looks right and she stays on the floor line.
 3. Wait for her 3rd attack (she does swipe, swipe, slam). Watch: warning slam (no damage) -> second slam.
    a) stand still in front of her -> you should be hit (-15)
    b) jump over her before the 2nd slam and land behind her -> no damage
    c) be in the air when the 2nd slam lands -> no damage
    d) landing back in the sparks while they are still on screen counts as a hit (by design)
    Tell me if anything looks like "standing in the sparks but not hurt" (or the reverse).
 4. Beat the Chain Goth: new death plays; she lies still, then fades.
 5. Let her knock you out: "ugh" then thud as you land; the 'taken' cutscene follows.
 6. Map: zoom is closer than the old default but less blurry than 1.7.
