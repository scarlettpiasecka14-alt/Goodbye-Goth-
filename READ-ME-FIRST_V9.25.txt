THE FIGHT
 - Patricia has 300 HP (Olly's 100 HP as before). Her art: walk, idle, sword lunge, sword combo, lightning, purple-smoke vanish, hurt, defeat.
 - STAGE 1 (300 -> 150 HP), in this order: sword LUNGE (12), sword COMBO (3 hits x 8), VANISH STRIKE (15), COMBO, LUNGE, repeat.
 - At 150 HP she staggers into STAGE 2 ("No more Mrs Nice Goth"): she moves a little faster, attacks sooner, and
   LIGHTNING SLAM (25) joins the pattern. Stage 2 is also your checkpoint: if you are knocked out after that point,
   Retry restarts at Patricia 150 HP with a 3-2-1. If you are knocked out in stage 1, Retry starts her at full health.
 - Ring colours: RED = lunge/combo (dodge or jump avoids it), PURPLE = vanish strike (she vanishes and the ring shows
   where she'll pop out, on the other side of Olly; dodge or jump), YELLOW = lightning (only JUMPING avoids it; being
   on the ground within the ring when the bolt lands = hit).
 - Normal punches never stagger her (she shrugs them off). A Heavy Attack staggers her only if she's between moves.
   While she is a cloud of smoke she can't be hit.
 - When she falls her "Salvagable?" animation plays, holds, then the Level 1 Complete screen appears as before.
 - The existing Patricia intro (talking, potion, drink, 3-2-1) is untouched; she now uses her ready pose through it.
