GOODBYE GOTH v8.3

Zombie Goth walk timing test.

Change in v8.3:
- Kept the same Zombie Goth walking artwork and sequence from v8.2.
- Changed the 12 walk frames to a uniform 150 ms per frame.
- This removes the small 100/110 ms timing variation that could make the middle of the walk feel like it accelerates.
- No AI, damage, attack, collision, Olly, map, or other gameplay changes were made.

Purpose:
Test whether a uniform, slower walk timing makes the Zombie Goth movement read as a steady walk.
